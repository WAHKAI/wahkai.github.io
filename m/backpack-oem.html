The terms, before you ask

Eight rules that decide whether an order is worth starting.

Minimum order
300 per design, or 50 on a stock style. Colourways of one design share the run.
Sampling
CLO 3D pattern first, then a physical prototype. Seven to ten days from the brief.
Revisions
A sample round turns in three to five days, and the pattern is edited, not re-made.
Bulk lead time
25–35 days from approved sample and deposit, across seven lines.
Stock orders
48 hours out of the warehouse, or 10–14 days once your logo goes on.
Shipping
FOB Guangzhou, or CIF and door-to-door DDP to 50+ countries.
Inspection
AQL 2.5 on the finished lot, 100% piece-by-piece on request.
Your pattern
Measured, rebuilt in 3D, and kept out of our catalogue. It is not resold to another buyer.

How an order runs

Four stages, with the days we quote rather than a best case.

2–3 days

Artwork and costing

A reference bag or a tech pack goes in. A costed BOM, a 3D render and a fabric list come back.

7–10 days

Sample

CLO 3D pattern first, then a physical prototype. Revisions land in 3–5 days.

25–35 days

Production

Seven lines, layered cutting, an in-line check at every station, AQL 2.5 on the finished lot.

3–5 days

Inspection and dispatch

Final audit and carton photos, then loading. Stock styles skip it and leave in 48 hours.

Where the money sits while it runs

Two payments in four stages — the balance moves on photographs.

30% With the order

Proforma invoice first, carrying the BOM, the carton spec and the delivery date.

0% Sample approved

The sample is billed on its own and paid by PayPal or Western Union. Nothing reaches the line until you sign the prototype and the colour lab dips.

70% Before shipment

Falls due after the AQL 2.5 audit and the carton photos, so nothing is paid on a lot nobody has seen.

0% On the documents

Bill of lading, packing list, invoice and certificate of origin, aligned with your broker before anything is filed.

Questions buyers ask us

What is the minimum order for a custom backpack?

300 pieces per design, and 100 pieces on a ready-to-ship style. The 300 is per design, not per colourway, so two colours of the same backpack still count as one run.

Can you build a backpack from a photo, without a tech pack?

Yes, and it is how about a third of our programmes start. We need the photo, the dimensions you are working to, and the target landed cost. We come back with a pattern drawing and a BOM you can correct before anything is cut.

What size range do you run for hiking packs?

The shapes we currently build run from 25 L daypacks to 80 L trekking packs. Volume is the easy part — the harness is what decides whether a pack sells, so we quote the hip belt and shoulder straps as a separate line.

Do you make the harness and hip belt yourselves, or buy them in?

Cut and sewn in-house. EVA and PE foams are laminated and moulded on our own presses, so a harness change is a pattern change, not a supplier order with a 30-day lead time.

Can we start with one family and add the others later?

That is the usual order. A buyer takes laptop backpacks first, proves the shelf, then adds school or hiking on the same pattern and the same fabric so the range looks like one brand rather than three suppliers.

How much faster is stock than custom?

48 hours against 25–35 days. Stock is also the only way to test a logo before committing: branding a stock backpack takes 10–14 days at 100 pieces, with no tooling and no sample round.

Can you copy the pattern of a backpack we already sell?

Yes. Send the bag or a pattern with a BOM. We measure the existing unit, rebuild it in CLO 3D, and send the screen model for approval before a sample is cut. Your pattern is not added to our catalogue.

Do you do private label on stock backpacks?

Yes — woven label, rubber patch, embroidery or thermal print, at the 100-piece minimum. The bag itself stays a shared style, so other buyers can still order the same shape in their own colours.
