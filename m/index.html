ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Have a different concern? Ask us — we reply in 2–4 hours.

01What is your minimum order quantity?
100 units for a ready-to-ship style, 300 for a fully custom design. The 300 is per design, not per colourway — colourways can split across shared core fabrics. Smaller first runs are quoted case by case, so ask before you rule us out.
02Can you customise an existing style?
Yes. Every catalogue style can be rebranded — embroidery, silk-screen, rubber patches, custom lining, pullers, hangtags. Five customisation families hold 50 craft options and 500+ materials, from a small tweak to a new pattern.
03How long does it take from order to delivery?
7–10 days for the first prototype, 3–5 days per revision, then 25–35 days of production once the sample is signed. Transit time depends on mode and destination, and the whole schedule is agreed in writing before the deposit is paid.
04How do you control quality?
Standard is AQL 2.5, inspected in-house with open access for SGS, Intertek or QIMA before balance payment. If your programme requires it, we switch to 100% piece-by-piece inspection — stitching, hardware, symmetry and labelling.
05What if the bulk order does not match the sample?
If bulk misses the sealed golden sample, we repair, replace or refund. The comparison is made against the physical bag you signed off, not a photograph, so the fix reflects what actually arrived in your warehouse, not what left ours.
