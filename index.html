About SANDA

Driven by design, powered by scale.

Founded in 2019 in Guangzhou, our 7,000 m² factory runs seven dedicated production lines and an in-house R&D team — 183 people, 135 machines, one roof.

We build custom-branded laptop, school, hiking, tote, duffle, sling and waist bags for global brands. Our own in-house designs are also ready to ship from stock. 50,000+ pieces a month, first samples in 7–10 days.

Factory Tour

Request a live video walkthrough
  1. 01

    Incoming Material QC

    Every roll of fabric and batch of trim is checked to spec before it reaches the line.

  2. 02

    Fabric Cutting

    Layered cutting across 7 lines, cut to the approved pattern for repeatable panels.

  3. 03

    Cut-Part Bundling

    Panels are counted and bundled by size and colour, so no mixed bundle reaches sewing.

  4. 04

    Hardware & Trims

    Zippers, buckles, webbing and lining are staged and counted per work order.

  5. 05

    Golden Sample

    The approved sample stays at the station as the reference operators check against.

  6. 10

    Zipper Function Test

    Every zipper is run end to end and every buckle cycled before final inspection.

  7. 09

    Pressing & Shaping

    Steam pressing sets the final form, so the bag holds its shape out of the carton.

  8. 08

    Assembly & Closing

    Lining, binding and final closing. Each bag is turned, checked and closed on the line.

  9. 07

    Stress-Point Stitching

    Handles, straps and base seams are bartacked and double-stitched — where cheap bags fail.

  10. 06

    Sewing Line

    Main assembly. Each operator runs one operation, keeping stitch quality consistent.

  11. 11

    In-Line Inspection

    Inspectors work on the line, not only at the end, so defects are caught early.

  12. 12

    Final Inspection

    AQL 2.5, or 100% on request. Stitching, hardware, symmetry and labelling.

  13. 13

    Load Testing

    Handles and straps are load-tested on every run, above the order's limit.

  14. 14

    Packing & Labelling

    Polybag, hangtag, carton and shipping mark applied to spec, then checked off the list.

  15. 15

    Warehouse & Shipping

    Finished goods ship to 50+ countries, with the paperwork your customs broker needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

01What is your minimum order quantity?

100 units for a ready-to-ship style, 300 for a fully custom design. The 300 is per design, not per colourway — colourways can split across shared core fabrics. Smaller first runs are quoted case by case, so ask before you rule us out.

02Can you customise an existing style?

Yes. Every catalogue style can be rebranded — embroidery, silk-screen, rubber patches, custom lining, pullers, hangtags. Five customisation families hold 50 craft options and 500+ materials, from a small tweak to a new pattern.

03Can you develop a bag from our own design?

Yes. Send a sketch or a tech pack under NDA. Our in-house team builds the pattern in CLO 3D first, so proportions and panel breaks are settled on screen before anything is cut. A physical prototype then follows in 7–10 days, not weeks.

04Will the design be exclusive to our brand?

Yes. NDAs are signed before tech-pack intake, CAD files stay on secure servers, and tooling is never shared between clients. A pattern developed for you is not resold to another buyer, and the design is not added to our catalogue.

05Do you make a sample before bulk production?

Always. Nothing enters production without your written, signed approval. The approved unit is sealed and kept at the station as the golden reference, and bulk is checked against that bag — not against a drawing or a screen render.

06How long does it take from order to delivery?

7–10 days for the first prototype, 3–5 days per revision, then 25–35 days of production once the sample is signed. Transit time depends on mode and destination, and the whole schedule is agreed in writing before the deposit is paid.

07How do you control quality?

Standard is AQL 2.5, inspected in-house with open access for SGS, Intertek or QIMA before balance payment. If your programme requires it, we switch to 100% piece-by-piece inspection — stitching, hardware, symmetry and labelling.

08What are the payment terms?

30% T/T deposit with the 70% balance before shipment. L/C at sight for large programmes, and PayPal or Western Union for sample fees, so a prototype never requires a bank transfer. All terms are confirmed on the proforma invoice.

09Can you handle shipping and customs?

Door-to-door to 50+ countries. Form E and certificate-of-origin details are aligned with your broker before filing, wood packaging is ISPM-15 heat-treated, Amazon FBA prep — suffocation labels, ASIN barcodes, carton marks — is done in-house.

10What if the bulk order does not match the sample?

If bulk misses the sealed golden sample, we repair, replace or refund. The comparison is made against the physical bag you signed off, not a photograph, so the fix reflects what actually arrived in your warehouse, not what left ours.
