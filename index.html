About SANDA

Driven by design, powered by scale.

Founded in 2019 in Guangzhou, our 7,000 m² factory runs seven dedicated production lines and an in-house R&D team — 183 people, 135 machines, one roof.

We build custom-branded laptop, school, hiking, tote, duffle, sling and waist bags for global brands. Our own in-house designs are also ready to ship from stock. 50,000+ pieces a month, first samples in 7 days.

Factory Tour

Request a live video walkthrough
  1. 01

    Incoming Material QC

    Every roll of fabric and batch of trim is checked to spec before it reaches the line.

  2. 02

    Fabric Cutting

    Layered cutting across 7 lines, cut to the approved pattern for repeatable panels.

  3. 03

    Cut-Part Bundling

    Panels are counted and bundled by size and colour, so no mixed bundle reaches sewing.

  4. 04

    Hardware & Trims

    Zippers, buckles, webbing and lining are staged and counted per work order.

  5. 05

    Golden Sample

    The approved sample stays at the station as the reference operators check against.

  6. 10

    Zipper Function Test

    Every zipper is run end to end and every buckle cycled before final inspection.

  7. 09

    Pressing & Shaping

    Steam pressing sets the final form, so the bag holds its shape out of the carton.

  8. 08

    Assembly & Closing

    Lining, binding and final closing. Each bag is turned, checked and closed on the line.

  9. 07

    Stress-Point Stitching

    Handles, straps and base seams are bartacked and double-stitched - where cheap bags fail.

  10. 06

    Sewing Line

    Main assembly. Each operator runs one operation, keeping stitch quality consistent.

  11. 11

    In-Line Inspection

    Inspectors work on the line, not only at the end, so defects are caught early.

  12. 12

    Final 100% Check

    Every unit is inspected, not a sample. Stitching, hardware, symmetry and labelling.

  13. 13

    Load Testing

    Handles and straps are load-tested on every run, above the order's limit.

  14. 14

    Packing & Labelling

    Polybag, hangtag, carton and shipping mark applied to spec, then checked off the list.

  15. 15

    Warehouse & Shipping

    Finished goods ship to 50+ countries, with the paperwork your customs broker needs.

Buyer Concerns & SANDA Solutions

Have a different concern? Ask us

Every stage of an order, and how we de-risk it — in the words of the importers who raised it.

MOQ
We need to test regional market demand with low starting volumes across multiple colorways.

— Sporting goods chain · Uzbekistan

SANDA Solution

300-unit minimum runs per design, with split colourways on shared core fabrics.

Tooling
High import taxes make high-cost tooling and custom metal molds financially unfeasible.

— Domestic luggage brand · Brazil

SANDA Solution

A large open-mold hardware catalogue cuts upfront tooling while still allowing embossed logos.

Sampling
Our launch will fail if the sample-making cycle drags on for weeks without fast revision.

— DTC lifestyle brand · South Korea

SANDA Solution

In-house CLO 3D pattern modelling: first physical prototype in 7–10 days, revisions in 3–5.

Confidentiality
Our custom tech pack might be shared or leaked to competitors before our collection launches.

— Streetwear label · United States

SANDA Solution

Mutual bilateral NDAs before tech-pack intake; CAD files on secure servers, molds never shared.

US Safety
Failure to comply with California Proposition 65 or CPSIA lead limits risks major legal fines.

— Retail brand · United States

SANDA Solution

Traceable ICP-MS lab records confirming lead below 90 ppm — Prop 65 and CPSIA ready.

EU Chemicals
European customs will block our goods if materials do not comply with REACH SVHC rules.

— Workwear supplier · Germany

SANDA Solution

Certified lab testing on every fabric and trim for zero SVHC phthalates, azo dyes and PFAS.

Recycled Content
We need verified Global Recycled Standard certification to validate our sustainability claims.

— Eco-conscious brand · United Kingdom

SANDA Solution

GRS transaction certificates for rPET and post-consumer nylon, auditable from yarn to bag.

Inspection
Production defects found after the container arrives overseas leave us with unsellable stock.

— Luggage importer · United States

SANDA Solution

AQL 2.5 inspection in-house, plus open access for SGS, Intertek or QIMA before balance payment.

Humidity
Our high-humidity climate causes mold and odor issues in fabric during long ocean transit.

— Outdoor retail distributor · Vietnam

SANDA Solution

Anti-mold desiccants and breathable PE liners in every carton, with moisture-tested fabric checks.

Water Resistance
We struggle to find water-resistant fabric that handles monsoon rain without adding weight.

— Commuter gear brand · Singapore

SANDA Solution

210T silnylon and coated ripstop giving IPX4–IPX6 protection with welded or taped seams.

Heat & UV
Intense heat, UV exposure and desert dust degrade PU coatings and cause fabric fading.

— Travel accessories distributor · UAE

SANDA Solution

UV-stabilized thread, colorfast dyes and temperature-resistant TPU to resist cracking and peeling.

Fabric Consistency
Bulk production may show lower fabric density and weaker tear-strength than the sample.

— Outdoor expedition brand · France

SANDA Solution

Calibrated tensile-pull QC tests denier and tear strength against the approved golden sample.

Communication
Language gaps and time delays make change requests during production risky.

— Trade procurement firm · Kyrgyzstan

SANDA Solution

One dedicated account manager, bilingual documentation, daily photo tracking, milestone sign-offs.

Capacity
A sudden volume spike of 30,000 units before holiday peak could overwhelm factory capacity.

— Incentive gifts · United States

SANDA Solution

7 dedicated lines at 30,000 units monthly, with dual-shift surge reaching 50,000.

Deadlines
Shipping delays frequently cause late arrivals for fixed trade show and event deadlines.

— Events agency · Netherlands

SANDA Solution

Backward-planned timelines with buffer windows, tracking port cut-offs and daily sewing progress.

Branding
We need custom corporate branding applied fast on off-the-shelf styles for regional events.

— Promotional gifts · Saudi Arabia

SANDA Solution

Ready-to-brand blanks in stock, with debossed patches, thermal prints or embroidery in 10–14 days.

Carton Strength
Rough overland transit through container terminals breaks zippers and damages cartons.

— Luggage wholesaler · Kazakhstan

SANDA Solution

Reinforced 5-ply double-corrugated cartons, corner protectors and stress-tested #8/#10 zippers.

Port Handling
Port congestion and rough port handling frequently rupture thin packing cartons.

— Retail wholesale importer · Kenya

SANDA Solution

Heavy shrink wrap, edge corner boards and reinforced strapping through every multi-leg transfer.

Biosecurity
Strict biosecurity checks can seize shipments with natural materials or wood packaging.

— Adventure gear company · Australia

SANDA Solution

ISPM-15 certified heat-treated pallets and moisture-free synthetic desiccants throughout.

Freight Cost
High sea freight rates eat into margins when bags are packed with too much empty dead space.

— DTC travel goods brand · Sweden

SANDA Solution

Flat-folding patterns and custom-sized master cartons maximise container fill and cut cost per bag.

Customs Docs

HS codes, descriptions and weights pre-aligned with your broker before Form E is filed.

Local Clearance

Clean Spanish/English commercial invoices, packing lists and B/L descriptions matched to your agent.

Marketplace Prep

Complete FBA prep in-house: suffocation prints, ASIN barcodes and master carton labelling.

Guarantee
Overseas wire transfers without guaranteed after-sales support carry real commercial risk.

A signed agreement: if bulk misses the sealed golden sample, we repair, replace or refund.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. OEM & ODM & Logo?

100% full-service manufacturer. We customize structures, branding (embroidery, silk-screen, rubber), and bespoke packaging.

2. Materials & Capability?

Specialized in premium outdoor/commuter bags. We process waterproof TPU/PVC, multi-layer cooler structures, and GRS RPET.

3. MOQ & Pricing?

Standard MOQ is 300 pcs/style. Tiered pricing based on volume and materials. Contact us for flexible solutions for growing brands.

4. Lead Time?

Physical samples in 7-10 days. Mass production bulk orders delivered within 25-35 days after sample approval.

5. Quality Control?

Strict 4-step internal QC with 100% inspection. Guaranteed <0.5% defect rate. We support SGS/BSCI third-party testing.

6. Payment Terms?

30% T/T deposit, 70% balance before shipment. L/C at sight for large orders. PayPal/Western Union for samples.
