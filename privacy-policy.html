Who we are

SANDA Backpack Manufacturing Co., Ltd. (“SANDA”, “we”, “us”) designs and manufactures custom backpacks and bags. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

We only collect what you type into our quote form:

  • Your name — so we know who we are replying to.
  • Your work email address — to send the quotation and catalog.
  • Your phone number (optional) — only if you prefer a call or WhatsApp.
  • Your message — the styles, materials, quantities or artwork you describe, including any file or image you choose to paste into that field.

We also receive standard technical information that any web server records, such as the page you requested and the time of the request.

We do not ask for, and do not want, payment card numbers, government ID numbers, health information, or any other sensitive category of personal data. Please do not send them through the form.

Why we use it

We use your data for one purpose: to answer your enquiry and, if you become a customer, to fulfil the order you place.

  • To reply to you. Your name, email, phone and message are used to prepare a quotation and send it back.
  • To produce your order. Designs, artwork and specifications are used to manufacture and ship what you ordered.
  • To keep the legally required records. Invoices and contracts are retained as required by accounting and tax law.

We do not sell your data, rent it, or add you to a marketing list you did not ask for. We do not send newsletters to form submissions unless you explicitly ask to be kept informed.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies of its own.

We do not run advertising trackers. The site loads the Inter typeface from Google Fonts, which means Google receives the visitor’s IP address as part of that request. No cookies are set by that request, and no analytics or remarketing pixel is installed.

If you embed or open one of our videos or social profiles (Facebook, TikTok), that platform applies its own cookies and privacy policy once you are on its site. Those are outside our control.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; nothing on this site depends on them.

Who else sees it

Your data is seen by our own sales and production staff, and by the service providers that make the site work:

  • FormSubmit — relays the quote form to our email inbox. Our quote form is delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form-to-email relay. Your form data passes through that service on its way to our inbox.
  • Google Fonts — serves the typeface used on every page.
  • Google Maps — renders the map in the contact section. The map is lazy-loaded, so it only makes a request once it scrolls into view.
  • Our email provider — stores the messages we receive and send.

We disclose data to authorities only where we are legally obliged to, and we will tell you unless the law forbids it.

Where your data goes

We are based in Guangzhou, China, and our staff read enquiries there. Some of the providers listed above process data outside China — Google Fonts and Google Maps, for example, serve requests from global infrastructure.

By sending us an enquiry you understand that your data will be handled in China and, where a provider requires it, in the provider’s own jurisdiction.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become orders — kept up to 24 months, so that a conversation can be picked up again, then deleted.
  • Quotations and order records — kept for the period required by accounting and tax law after the order closes.
  • Artwork and tooling files — kept for as long as we hold your tooling, and returned or destroyed on request.

Your rights

Write to peter@sanda2019.com.cn and we will action any of the following, normally within 30 days:

  • Access — a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — fixing anything that is wrong or out of date.
  • Deletion — erasing your enquiry and our replies, except records we must keep by law.
  • Objection or restriction — asking us to stop using your data for a purpose.
  • Portability — a machine-readable export of what you gave us.

If you are in the EU or UK, you also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority. We would rather you came to us first.

Security

Access to enquiry data is limited to the staff who need it. We use HTTPS for this site and keep order files in access-controlled storage. No system is perfect: if a breach ever affected your data, we would tell you and the relevant authority as required.

Children

This is a business-to-business site. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we update this page and the date at the top. Material changes that affect existing enquiries are also communicated by email to the address you gave us.
